Abstract
This paper addresses decision-making problems in the production process by establishing a stratified random sampling inspection model and providing the optimal sampling inspection plan; a multi-stage decision-making model is established, and the specific optimal decision-making plans for various situations are given; a multi-process and multi-stage decision-making model is established, and the optimal decision-making plan under multiple processes is provided; a sampling inspection simulation model is established, and the optimal decision-making plans for problem one and problem two are given.

For problem one, a stratified sampling inspection model is established, and the minimum sampling inspection times under 90% and 95% confidence levels are provided. Firstly, under stable conditions, considering that the inspection results of each component are independent and the defect rate remains consistent, a binomial distribution model is established to fit the stratified sampling inspection process. Then, on this basis, a hypothesis testing model is established to comprehensively evaluate the reliability of the components. Next, the established stratified sampling inspection model is solved using MATLAB, and the corresponding optimal inspection plans are determined under different allowable errors. For instance, when the allowable error is 0.05, the minimum inspection times are 98 under a 95% confidence level and 59 under a 90% confidence level. Finally, a sensitivity analysis of the minimum inspection times is conducted, revealing that the minimum inspection times are not sensitive to the nominal value, indicating a high robustness of the model.

For problem two, a multi-stage decision-making model is established, and the optimal decision-making plans for various situations, along with the decision-making basis and index results, are provided. Firstly, the production stages of the enterprise are visualized to make the entire production process clear and understandable. Then, based on the 0-1 integer programming model, a multi-stage decision-making model is established to conduct a detailed analysis of each stage, deriving the mathematical expressions for the pass rate, defect rate, and cost of each stage, and determining the expression for the total cost. Next, the exhaustive method is used to conduct a comprehensive search of the decision-making paths, with the goal of minimizing the total cost, to determine the optimal decision-making plans for each situation, and the decision-making basis for each stage is determined using the control variable method. For example, the optimal decision-making plan for situation 1 is plan 16, with a total cost of 2380 yuan. Other situations are detailed in Table 6. Finally, a Monte Carlo test is conducted on the model, and a comparative analysis shows that the addition of random defect rate variations has a small fluctuation and is more in line with reality than the non-random defect rate, indicating that the decision-making plans for this problem are effective.

For problem three, a multi-process and multi-stage decision-making model is established, and the optimal decision-making plan under multiple processes, along with the decision-making basis and index results, are provided. Firstly, similar to problem two, based on the 0-1 integer programming model, a sub-process planning model is established, and the mathematical expressions for the pass rate, defect rate, and cost of each stage under three component situations are derived. Then, combining the conclusions of problem two, the expression for the total cost is determined. Next, the exhaustive method is used to solve the model, and the optimal decision-making plan is determined as: no inspection for all components and semi-finished products. The decision-making basis for each stage is determined using the control variable method. Finally, a perturbation test is conducted on the total cost, revealing that the defect rate has almost no impact on the total cost, indicating a high robustness of the model established for this problem.

For problem four, a sampling inspection simulation model is established, and the optimal decision-making plans for problem two and problem three are updated. Firstly, based on the binomial distribution of problem one, the posterior functions of components, finished products, and semi-finished products are derived using Bayesian theory. Then, based on the mixed integer programming model and combining the cost expressions of problem one and problem two, the mathematical expression for the total cost is derived. Then, the whale algorithm was applied in combination with the cutting plane method and the branch and bound method to solve the model. Based on the decision-making criteria determined in problems two and three, the updated optimal solutions for problems two and three were identified. For instance, in problem two, situation 1's optimal decision-making plan was: no inspection of components, inspection of finished products, and disassembly of non-conforming finished products; for problem three, the optimal decision-making plan was: no inspection of all components and semi-finished products, no disassembly of semi-finished products 1 and 2, disassembly of semi-finished product 3, inspection of finished products, and no disassembly of finished products. Finally, the optimal parameters of the whale algorithm were calculated using the comparative analysis method, enhancing the objectivity of the results.

摘  要
本文针对生产过程中的决策问题，建立了分层随机抽样检测模型，给出了最优抽样检测方案；建立了多阶段决策模型，给出了各个情况的具体最优决策方案；建立了多工序多阶段决策模型，给出了多工序下的最优决策方案；建立了抽样检测仿真模型，给出了问题一和问题二的最优决策方案。

针对问题一，建立了分层抽样检测模型，给出了90%和95%的信度下的最少抽样检测次数方案。首先，在稳定条件下，考虑到每个零配件的检测结果是独立的且次品率保持一致，建立了二项分布模型来拟合分层抽样检测过程。然后，在此基础上，建立了假设检验模型，全面评估了零配件的可靠性。接着，运用MATLAB对建立的分层抽样检测模型进行求解，在不同的允许误差下，确定了相应的最优检测方案。例如，当允许误差为0.05时，在95%的信度下最少检测次数为98，在90%的信度下最少检测次数为59。最后，对最少检测次数进行灵敏度分析，发现最少检测次数对标称值不敏感，表明模型的稳健程度较高。

针对问题二，建立了多阶段决策模型，给出了各个情况的最优决策方案以及决策依据和指标结果。首先，将企业生产各个阶段进行可视化处理，让整个生产过程清晰明了。然后，以0-1整数规划模型为基础，建立了多阶段决策模型，对各个阶段进行精细化分析，推导出了各个阶段的合格率、次品率和成本的数学表达式，确定了总成本的表达式。接着，运用穷举法对决策路径进行全面搜索，以总成本最小为目标，确定了各情况下的最优决策方案，并运用控制变量法确定每个阶段的决策依据。例如，情况1的最优决策方案为方案16，总花费成本为2380元，其他情况具体见表 6。最后，对模型进行蒙特卡洛检验，对比分析得出，添加随机性次品率变化浮动较小，且比未添加随机性次品率更契合实际，表明本题的决策方案效果较好。

针对问题三，建立了多工序多阶段决策模型，给出了多工序下的最优决策方案以及决策依据和指标结果。首先，与问题二类似，以0-1整数规划模型为基础，建立了子工序规划模型，推导出了三个零部件情况下的各阶段合格率、次品率和成本的数学表达式。然后，结合问题二的结论，确定了总成本的表达式。接着，运用穷举法进行求解，确定了最优决策方案为：全部零部件和半成品均未检测，并运用控制变量法确定了每个阶段的决策依据。最后，对总成本进行扰动检验，发现次品率几乎不对总成本造成扰动，说明本题建立的模型鲁棒性较高。

针对问题四，建立了抽样检测仿真模型，更新了问题二和问题三的最优决策方案。首先，在问题一的二项分布基础上，运用贝叶斯理论，推导出了零配件、成品和半成品的后验函数。然后，以混合整数规划模型为基础，结合问题一和问题二的成本表达式，推导出了总成本的数学表达式。接着，运用鲸鱼算法，结合割平面法和分支定界法对模型进行求解，以问题二和问题三所确定的决策依据为支撑，确定了问题二和问题三更新后的最优方案，例如：问题二中情况1的最优决策方案为：零部件均不检测、检测成品、拆解不合格成品；问题三的最优决策方案为：全部零部件和半成品均未检测，半成品1和2未拆解，半成品3拆解，成品检测，成品未拆解。最后，运用对比分析法计算出了鲸鱼算法的最优参数，增强了结果的客观性。
